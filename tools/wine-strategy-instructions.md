## The public wine site

The Notion Wine Library feeds Jake's public wine site (https://jakeworcester-byte.github.io/wine-library/) and its Ask Jake chat. A sync copies Notion to the site every 30 minutes, so changes in Notion go live on their own:

- **Pulling a bottle:** lower On Hand and remove that bottle's slot from Location. At 0 the wine leaves the cellar page and moves to the tasting record.
- **Adding a bottle:** a new row with On Hand and Location appears right away; a weekly routine adds the photo and tasting notes.

Notes, Price Paid, Buy Ceiling and Source never leave Notion. Guests only see three columns of text, so fill them whenever you log or update a wine:

- **Guest Note:** one or two short sentences in Jake's first-person voice about how the wine drank. Warm and specific. Keep comparisons to other vintages and trip or dinner context ("tasted at the winery in August 2026"). If Glass Evolution is "Built," end with "Kept building in the glass." For a buy-list wine not yet tasted, say why it's on the list. A Tuesday Night score in the high 80s is a good bottle doing its job, not a knock.
- **Guest Pairing:** a few words, only for a real pairing (for example "Prime ribeye").
- **Guest Flag:** a short caution on a cellar bottle guests shouldn't open, for example "Saved for a vertical tasting."

Never put prices, buy ceilings, stores, "under market," scoring-system talk (V5, lanes, calibration, data points) or cellar logistics in a Guest column, and never use em dashes. If a wine has no tasting impressions worth sharing, put "-" in Guest Note. If you're unsure, leave Guest Note empty: the weekly routine writes it from Notes.
